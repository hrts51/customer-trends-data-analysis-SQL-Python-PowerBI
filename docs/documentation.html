💰 9. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
CLV = purchase_amount × (365 / purchase_frequency_days) × lifespan_years

lifespan_years = 1 + (previous_purchases / purchases_per_year) / 12

AlgorithmGradient Boosting Regressor

R² Score0.9999

🔍 16. Key Business Insights
1
Discounts Are Statistically Ineffective — A/B test shows discount buyers don't spend more (p=0.27, d=0.04). The discount program erodes margin without driving incremental revenue.
2
Subscriptions Predict Retention, Not Ticket Size — Subscribed customers have more previous purchases but don't spend significantly more per transaction (p=0.66). Subscription drives frequency, not spend.
3
CLV is Highly Predictable — Gradient Boosting predicts CLV with R²=0.9999. Previous purchases and purchase frequency are the dominant drivers — engagement history is everything.
4
Champions Are the Revenue Core — Top RFM segment (~20% of customers) generates a disproportionate revenue share. Retaining Champions should be the top business priority.
5
Churn Profile is Clear — At-risk customers: infrequent (≥90 days), low rating (<3.5), not subscribed. These can be directly targeted with personalised re-engagement campaigns.
6
Clothing + Spring = Peak Revenue — Clothing is the dominant category across all seasons. Spring consistently peaks — seasonal inventory planning should be concentrated here.
7
Gender Spend Gap is Negligible — Male customers are 68% of transactions but the avg spend difference vs female is statistically insignificant (p=0.38). Gender-based pricing/targeting is not justified by data.
8
Revenue is Geographically Dispersed — No single state dominates. Revenue is spread evenly across 50 states — a national rather than regional strategy is appropriate.
